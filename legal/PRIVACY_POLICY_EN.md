## 1. What FOLIO is

FOLIO is a mobile app that provides **preliminary legal information only**, generated by an AI model. The app does not provide personal legal advice, does not substitute a licensed attorney, and does not establish an attorney-client relationship.

## 2. What we collect

Your data is stored **on your device only**, unless you choose to create an authenticated account (optional). Possible data:

| Data type | Stored where | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Name you entered at onboarding | Device (AsyncStorage) | UI personalization |
| Legal questions you submit | Device; transmitted to the AI provider for processing | Generating an initial legal answer |
| Generated answers | Device (last 200 records) | History and lawyer-pack sharing |
| Checklist progress | Device | Progress tracking |
| Email + password (only if you create an account) | Supabase Auth | Login and cross-device sync |
| Aggregate usage events (analytics) | Supabase | Product improvement |
| Disclaimer acceptance | Device | Proof of informed consent |

**We do NOT collect:** Location, camera, contacts, advertising identifiers, call log, or information about other apps on your device.

## 3. AI provider transmission

When you ask a question, the **question text** is transmitted via encrypted HTTPS from the FOLIO back-end to our AI provider (currently OpenRouter, or alternatively Google Gemini). The provider may process the text under its own terms of service and does not retain conversational memory across requests.

**Provider policies:**
- OpenRouter: <https://openrouter.ai/privacy>
- Google AI: <https://ai.google/responsibility/principles>

**Do NOT include in your question:** Full national ID number, bank account number, credit-card numbers, passwords, or sensitive personal data not strictly necessary for the question.

## 4. Consent and deletion

- Consent is given at onboarding via an explicit checkbox.
- You can delete any individual saved answer from the Archive screen → long-press → "Delete".
- You can wipe all personal data via About → "Delete all data".
- If you have an account: sign out via About → "Sign out". Full account deletion: email us at the address above and we will remove the data within 30 days.

## 5. Security

- All traffic between the app and our back-end is encrypted with TLS (HTTPS).
- Passwords (account mode) are managed by Supabase Auth with hashed storage.
- AI provider API keys and the Supabase service-role key are never stored in the client app. They live only on the back-end server.
- We do not sell or rent your data to any third party.

## 6. Children

FOLIO is intended for users aged 13 and over. If you are under that age, do not use the app without a parent's involvement.

## 7. Legal disclaimer

The information in the app is **preliminary legal information only** and does not constitute legal advice. For urgent matters (criminal, violence, eviction, custody, dismissal), contact a licensed attorney immediately, or for emergencies the local police, ambulance, or dedicated hotlines.

## 8. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be shown in-app and will require renewed consent.
